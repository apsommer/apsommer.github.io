# Account & Data Deletion

## SignalVoice

SignalVoice allows optional account login using federated providers (e.g., Google). If you would like to delete your account and any associated data, you can request deletion using the instructions below.

---

## How to Request Deletion

Please send an email to:

**[support@signalvoice.com](mailto:support@signalvoice.com)**

Include the following:

* The email address used to sign in
* The subject line: **"Delete account"**

---

## What Happens Next

* Your request will be processed within **7 days**
* Any associated user data will be deleted from our systems
* You will receive confirmation once deletion is complete

---

## Data That May Be Deleted

If you have used login features, this may include:

* Account identifiers (e.g., email address)
* Associated app usage tied to your account (if applicable)

---

## No Account?

If you have not created an account or used login:

* SignalVoice does **not store personally identifiable account data**
* No action is required
