BarAudio delivers real-time trading alerts through a voice-first Android application. TradingView webhooks trigger a Firebase backend that streams high-signal events to the mobile client, where alerts are spoken using a foreground service.

I am Drew Sommer, an Android engineer focused on real-time mobile systems built with Kotlin, Jetpack Compose, and Firebase. My primary project, BarAudio, delivers spoken trading alerts to mobile devices using TradingView webhooks, a Firebase backend, and a voice-first Android client. Previously I contributed to the Electrify America Android application supporting EV charging infrastructure across North America. Before software engineering I spent nearly a decade as a mechanical engineer specializing in machine dynamics and vibration analysis, a background that continues to influence how I approach systems design and debugging.
